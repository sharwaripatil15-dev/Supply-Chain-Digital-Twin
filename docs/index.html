    HOW IT WORKS

    From "something broke" to "here is the safe fix"

    Throw a disruption at the twin (a plant fire, a flooded port, a festival demand spike) and watch the ripple travel day by day. AEGIS then finds the weak spots on its own, recommends a recovery plan with the trade-offs laid out, and tells you where a new warehouse or plant would pay off.

    THREE THINGS NOBODY ELSE SHOWS YOU

    The parts that make it different

    Move your cursor over a card.

    🔍

    Chaos Finder

    A reverse stress test. AEGIS knocks out every node and every pair and ranks the damage, surfacing hidden pairs: two sites that each look safe alone but together cut off a region.

    synergy = unmet(a+b) − unmet(a) − unmet(b)

    ⏱️

    Survival Clock

    One number a COO understands: days until the first customer runs out of stock. Under it: revenue at risk, plus P50/P90 from a Monte Carlo over uncertain outage durations.

    🛡️

    Fix-safety check

    The cheapest fix is often a trap: draining safety stock fixes today and creates tomorrow's single point of failure. Every plan is re-stress-tested after it is applied; the recommendation is the cheapest safe plan.
